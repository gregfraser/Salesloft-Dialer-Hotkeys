End call & log The red ✕ button

Click a key, then press the one you want — any key, number pad included. These work on the Salesloft page and in the floating panel, and are ignored while you are typing in a field.

To dial from another tab, Chrome runs the same two jobs from its own shortcuts. Chrome will not take a number pad key there, and it leaves a shortcut unset when something else already uses it:
